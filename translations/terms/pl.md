## Ambiguous strings

- "Tells visitors apart on the publisher's site." / "for reporting": "odróżnia", kept literal.
- "Rolls out player updates.": read as deploying player updates ("Wdraża").
- "Shows whether stored user attributes are current.": translated literally.
- "Records which campaign or website brought you here...": rendered impersonally, "you" dropped.
