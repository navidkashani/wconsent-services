## Ambiguous strings

- "Stores the signed-in user." Read as the login identity: "Guarda quem tem sessão iniciada."
- "Stores the group the visitor belongs to." Read as a visitor segment/group.
- "Shows ads to past visitors." Read as remarketing.
- "Tells devices apart." / "Tells browsers apart." Read as telling devices/browsers apart for identification.
- "Syncs the clock." Read as time synchronisation of the service.
- "Rolls out player updates." Read as gradual release of updates.
- "Avoids showing the same content twice." Read as content-repeat suppression.
